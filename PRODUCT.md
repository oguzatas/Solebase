# Notes (Prompt2UI example)

## Register

product

## Users

People who keep a personal Markdown vault locally, with optional encrypted sync. Primary job: write and find notes quickly without fighting chrome.

## Product Purpose

A quiet Obsidian-like notes app on Prompt2UI IndexedDB + E2EE settings. The page is the note; navigation and meta hide until needed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dense, trustworthy. Tool disappears into the task. No marketing surfaces inside the vault.

## Anti-references

- Dual left rails / app-shell chrome beside the vault
- Text-button toolbars and filter dropdown stacks
- Purple SaaS gradients, cream-serif terracotta, broadsheet rules
- Decorative glass, glow, emoji chrome

## Strategic Principles

1. Editor is the product surface; drawers for list and context.
2. One search field for find + filter (`#tag`, `path:`).
3. Icon actions with titles/ARIA; no labeled button rows.
4. Restrained dark neutrals + one sage accent ≤10%.
